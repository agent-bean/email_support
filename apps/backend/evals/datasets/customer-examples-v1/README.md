# Customer examples review guide

These six situations come from the owner-provided screenshot. Each has an English and Vietnamese case. Sender addresses are synthetic reserved addresses; no identity mapping is assumed. Company websites are reproduced as sender-provided input, not verified research evidence. There are no invented calendar events, attachment contents or public-source facts. All expectations are candidates awaiting owner review.

The current replay can score relevance only. Read-only search and source fetching can be exercised separately for the six companies when explicitly approved for that run, but replay does not score research quality. The requested briefing, identity, attachment, calendar, delivery and knowledge effects below need their respective production workflow and evidence; they are explicitly unsupported in the case records. Sending and knowledge promotion require their applicable approval controls.
